Fuel your body, own your day!

Your body depends on the food you eat.
The nutrients you consume help power your brain, muscles, heart, and immune system.

Benefits of Good Nutrition

  • Energy throughout the day
  • Stronger immune system
  • Better focus and concentration
  • Healthy weight Management
  • Stronger muscles nad Bones
  • Faster recovery after exercise
  • Lower risk of chronic diseases

The Balanced Plate!

A healthy meal includes:

🥬 50% Fruits and Vegetables
🍗 25% Lean Protein
🌾 25% Whole Grains
🥑 Healthy Fats in Moderation
💧 Plenty of Water Small improvements at each meal can lead to long-term health benefits.

👩‍⚕️ When to Seek Professional Advice

Consider speaking with a healthcare professional or registered dietitian if you:

✔ Feel tired most of the time
✔ Experience unexpected weight changes
✔ Have digestive concerns
✔ Think you may have a nutrient deficiency
✔ Need a personalized nutrition plan
✔ Have medical conditions that affect your diet
